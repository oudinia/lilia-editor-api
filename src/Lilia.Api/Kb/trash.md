# Delete, restore and empty the Trash

## Steps {#steps}
1. In **Documents**, on a document's card: **⋯ → Move to Trash**. **Undo** in the message brings it straight back.
2. Open the Trash: **Documents → Trash** (bottom of the list on the left), or the Lilia menu.
   ![The Trash](/help/img/trash/en-1.png)
3. Get a document back: **Restore**.
4. Delete it for good: **Delete Forever**. This can't be undone.
5. Documents in the Trash are deleted automatically after 30 days; each one shows how long it has left.

A document someone shared with you has **⋯ → Remove from my documents** instead: it leaves your list only.

## What this means {#beginner}
- Moving to the Trash is safe: nothing is lost for 30 days.
- **Delete Forever** really deletes, for you and for everyone you shared the document with.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- Tables you delete wait in the Trash too; templates, references, equations and snippets are deleted outright.
- Restoring puts the document back in Documents.
