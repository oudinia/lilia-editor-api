# Settings: your account and preferences

## Steps {#steps}
1. Open **Settings**: the avatar (top right) → **Settings**, or the Lilia menu. On a phone: the avatar on **Documents** → **Settings**.
2. Under **Account**:
   - **Profile**: the name others see on your comments, shares and presence. Change it, then **Save** in the bar that appears (**Discard** puts it back).
   - **Security**: **Change password**: your current password, then the new one. You stay signed in.
   - **Plan**: your plan and how much of it you've used this month.
   - **Connected apps**: AI apps you connected to Lilia, such as Claude. **Disconnect** stops one at once.
   - **Your data**: **Export everything**, and **Delete account…**.
3. Under **Preferences**:
   - **Appearance**: theme (System, Light, Dark), accent, language.
   - **Ask Lilia and help**: how Lilia explains things (Simple, Standard, For LaTeX users).
   - **Editor**: defaults for new documents, auto-save, LaTeX-native editing; **Troubleshooting** → **Clear cache & reload**.

## What this means {#beginner}
- Your email is the address you sign in with; it's shown in Profile but changes with your sign-in, not here.
- Appearance changes apply at once; nothing to save.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- Each section has its own address: /settings/profile, /settings/security, /settings/billing, /settings/apps, /settings/data.
- **LaTeX-native editing** (Editor) shows commands and source in the editor.
