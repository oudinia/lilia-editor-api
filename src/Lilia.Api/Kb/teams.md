# Work as a team

## Steps {#steps}
1. Open **Teams** (search *Teams* in the Lilia menu, ⌘K / Ctrl+K).
   ![Teams](/help/img/teams/en-1.png)
2. **New Team**, give it a name, then create it.
3. **Manage** → **Members**: enter a colleague's email, choose **Editor** or **Viewer**, and **Invite**.
4. Attach a document: in it, **Share** → **Team access** → attach to your team. Everyone in the team can now edit it.

## What this means {#beginner}
- A team saves you inviting the same people to every document: attach the document once.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- A document belongs to at most one team; attaching makes team members editors of it. Inviting someone directly still works alongside.
