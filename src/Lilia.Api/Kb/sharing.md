# Share a document with someone

## Steps {#steps}
1. In the document, click **Share** (top bar).
2. Under **Invite people**, type their email, choose **Can view** or **Can edit**, and click **Invite**.
   ![Invite people in the Share drawer](/help/img/sharing/en-1.png)
3. They get an email. Someone without a Lilia account sees the invitation as *pending* in your list for 14 days, with **Resend**, **Copy invite link** and **Cancel**.
4. To change someone's access, pick a new role next to their name. To stop sharing, choose **Remove from doc**: you can tell them by email, and **Undo** stays for a few seconds.
5. To let anyone read it without an account, turn on **Anyone with the link**, choose when it expires, and copy the link. **Revoke** turns it off; **Regenerate** gives a new link and stops the old one.
6. Working as a team? **Team access** attaches the document to your team: everyone in it can edit.

On a phone: **⋯ → Share**.

## What this means {#beginner}
- Nothing is shared until you invite someone or turn on the link.
- **Can view**: they read it, ask Lilia about it, and can make their own copy. **Can edit**: they change the document with you.
- An **invitation** is for one email address; only that person can use it. A **link** works for anyone who has it, so use an expiry date.
- Removing someone takes effect at once; links they saved stop working.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- Roles are *owner*, *editor* and *viewer* (*can comment* is coming). One owner per document.
- A viewer never gets edit tools, Ask Lilia's included: it answers but changes nothing.
- The public link is read-only (`/shared/…`); its expiry is set on the server, so an expired link fails for everyone.
- To hand someone the source instead, **Export → LaTeX** (a `.tex` or an Overleaf-style `.zip`).
