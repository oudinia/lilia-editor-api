# Lilia on your phone

## Steps {#steps}
1. Open Lilia in your phone's browser and sign in: you land on **Documents**, newest first (Today, This week, Earlier), with a search box.
   ![Documents on a phone](/help/img/phone/en-1.png)
2. Tap a document to read it. Tap a paragraph, then **Ask about this paragraph** to ask Lilia about it (or to change it, if you can edit).
3. The bar at the bottom asks Lilia about the whole document.
4. Top right: the outline, and **Preview the PDF**.
5. **⋯** has **Share**, **Check document**, **Look**, **Page & settings** and **Document details**.
6. Your avatar has **Appearance**, **Language**, **Settings** (Profile, Security, Plan, Connected apps, Your data) and **Sign out**.

## What this means {#beginner}
- A phone is for reading, checking and asking; you change the document through Ask Lilia.
- For writing at length, use a computer or a large tablet: the full editor opens there.
- Add Lilia to your home screen to open it like an app (your phone's browser menu → *Add to Home Screen*).

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- Tap a block, then **View LaTeX**: the source Lilia generates, with that block highlighted.
- Page settings changed on a phone (paper, columns, font size, margins…) save at once and offer **Undo** for a few seconds.
