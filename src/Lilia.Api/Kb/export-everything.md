# Export everything

## Steps {#steps}
1. Open **Settings → Your data**.
2. **Export everything**. Lilia prepares it in the background; you can leave the page. You get an email when it's ready.
3. Back in **Your data**: **Download**. The zip stays available for 7 days.
4. Want a fresh one later? **Make a new one**.

## What this means {#beginner}
- It's a copy: your documents stay in Lilia.
- Only you can download it, while signed in.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- Each document has its own folder: `document.json` (blocks, settings and bibliography as Lilia stores them) and `latex/` (the LaTeX project: main.tex, the .bib and images). `account.json` holds your profile.
- To export one document instead, use **Export** in the editor.
