# Delete or restore your account

## Steps {#steps}
1. If you want a copy, first **Settings → Your data → Export everything**.
2. **Settings → Your data → Delete account…**. The dialog says what goes: your documents, and the people who lose the ones you shared.
3. Type your account's email, enter your password, then **Delete my account**. You're signed out at once.
4. Changed your mind? Within 30 days, sign in again: Lilia shows **Your account is closed** → **Restore my account**. Everything comes back as it was.
5. After 30 days the account and its documents are erased.

## What this means {#beginner}
- Until it's erased, nothing is lost: restoring brings back every document.
- Documents you shared go for the people you shared them with too, so they may want a copy first.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- After erasure, comments you left on others' documents show "Deleted user".
- Deleting needs a recent sign-in; if Lilia asks you to sign in again, do so and come back to **Your data**.
