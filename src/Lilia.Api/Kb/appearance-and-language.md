# Change the theme, accent and language

## Steps {#steps}
1. Click your avatar (top right) → **Settings**, then **Appearance**.
   ![Settings, Appearance](/help/img/appearance-and-language/en-1.png)
2. **Theme**: **System** (follows your computer or phone, light by day and dark at night), **Light** or **Dark**.
3. **Accent**: **Cerulean**, **Plum** or **Ink**: the colour of buttons, links and selections.
4. **Light style (trying out)**: how light mode separates things (Crisp is the default).
5. **Language**: English, Français or Español. The page reloads in that language.

Changes apply at once, everywhere. Quicker: the moon/sun button in the top bar switches light and dark; the avatar menu's **Theme & accent…** opens the same choices. On a phone: avatar → **Appearance** and **Language**.

## What this means {#beginner}
- These settings change how Lilia looks to you, not how your paper looks. For the paper itself, see *Choose your paper's look*.
- Ask Lilia answers in the language you write in, whatever the interface's language.

## For LaTeX users {#expert}
- The interface language is separate from the document's language (set in its page settings), which controls hyphenation and LaTeX's own words (Contents, Figure, References).
